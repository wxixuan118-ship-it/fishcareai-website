Care baseline

The Corydoras panda, known for its distinctive white face and black body, is a popular freshwater fish. They are peaceful and make excellent additions to community tanks. They are moderately active and can thrive in a well-maintained environment. As bottom-dwellers, they require a tank with plenty of hiding spots.
