Care baseline

The Weather Loach, also known as the Weather Fish, is a popular addition to freshwater aquariums due to its peaceful nature and intriguing behavior. It has a slender, eel-like body with a translucent appearance and dark spots. These loaches are active and enjoy exploring their tank, making them a moderate activity level fish.
