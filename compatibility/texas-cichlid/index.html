Care baseline

The Texas Cichlid, known for its vibrant green and blue colors, is a striking addition to the freshwater aquarium. With a lifespan of up to 6 years, these fish are a popular choice among beginners and experienced aquarists alike. However, they are highly aggressive towards their own species and should be kept with non-aggressive tankmates.
