Choosing Rainbow Shark Tank Mates

Good Rainbow Shark tank mates share the same temperature and pH window, will not be eaten by (or eat) the Rainbow Shark, and do not compete for the same swimming zone or feeding time. Each pair guide below scores those factors from 0 to 100 and gives the minimum tank size for keeping both species together.

Start with the highest-scoring pairs; anything under 45 is listed so you know what to avoid, not as a recommendation.

Care baseline

Active bottom dweller that becomes territorial at maturity. One per tank; avoid other shark-like or bottom-dwelling species.
