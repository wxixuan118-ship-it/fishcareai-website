Care baseline

The Black Neon Tetra is a popular freshwater species known for its vibrant colors and peaceful temperament. It is an excellent addition to community tanks, thriving in groups of 6 or more. These tetras require a well-maintained aquarium with a flowing current and plenty of swimming space. With proper care, they can live up to 5 years.
