Care baseline

The Congo Tetra is a vibrant and active fish that thrives in a well-maintained freshwater aquarium. With its striking coloration and peaceful nature, it's a great addition to any community tank. This species is best kept in groups and requires a spacious tank with plenty of swimming space.

Temperature72–79°F
pH6.0–7.0
Adult size1.6 in
Minimum group6
