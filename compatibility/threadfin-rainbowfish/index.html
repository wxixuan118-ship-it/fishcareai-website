Care baseline

The Threadfin Rainbowfish is a popular choice for freshwater aquariums due to its vibrant colors and peaceful nature. It requires a well-maintained tank with plenty of swimming space and a moderate water flow. These fish are not known to be aggressive and can be kept in community tanks with other peaceful species.
