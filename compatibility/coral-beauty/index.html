Care baseline

The Coral Beauty Angelfish is a popular choice for reef tanks due to its vibrant colors and gentle demeanor. These fish thrive in well-maintained aquariums with plenty of swimming space and hiding spots. They are generally peaceful with other compatible fish but can be sensitive to aggressive tankmates. A diet rich in variety is essential for their health.
