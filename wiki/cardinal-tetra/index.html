Cardinal Tetra Care Guide 2026

Paracheirodon axelrodi — Schultz, 1956
🏠 Origin: Rio Negro & Orinoco, South America
📏 Size: 1.5–2 in (4–5 cm)
⏳ Lifespan: 4–5 years
⭐ Care level: Beginner–Intermediate

The cardinal tetra is widely considered the most beautiful schooling fish in freshwater aquariums. Unlike the neon tetra — whose red stripe covers only the rear half of the body — the cardinal's vivid scarlet stripe runs the entire body length, paired with an equally brilliant electric-blue iridescent stripe above. A school of 20 cardinal tetras in a blackwater planted tank is genuinely breathtaking.

Conservation note: Cardinal tetras from the Rio Negro are one of the world's most sustainably harvested ornamental fish — supporting thousands of indigenous Brazilian livelihoods and incentivizing forest conservation. Wild-caught cardinals are ecologically preferable in this unique case.

Natural Habitat

Cardinal tetras inhabit the blackwater tributaries of the Rio Negro and Orinoco — among the softest, most acidic environments on Earth. pH 4.0–5.5; GH nearly zero; temperature 77–82°F; water permanently stained dark brown by tannins. They school in vast numbers beneath Amazonian forest canopy.

Tank Setup

20-gallon minimum for 10 cardinals; 30–40 gallons for a display school of 20+. Classic blackwater setup: dark sand substrate, driftwood, Indian almond leaves releasing tannins, subdued LED lighting, and floating plants. The tank must be well-established (3+ months) before adding cardinals — they require stable, pristine conditions.

Diet

Micro-predators in the wild. Feed high-quality micro-pellets or fine flake twice daily; supplement with frozen baby brine shrimp, micro worms, cyclops, and daphnia. Their tiny mouths require appropriately sized food.

Compatible Tank Mates

Perfect companions for discus (identical water requirements), German blue rams, corydoras sterbai, dwarf gouramis, and other blackwater species. Avoid fish requiring hard alkaline water.
