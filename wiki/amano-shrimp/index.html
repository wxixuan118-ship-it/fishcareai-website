Amano Shrimp

Caridina multidentata · Amano shrimp are excellent algae grazers and are especially useful in planted aquariums. They are larger than cherry shrimp, but they still need peaceful fish and oxygen-rich, stable water.

Quick Answer

Amano shrimp are excellent algae grazers and are especially useful in planted aquariums. They are larger than cherry shrimp, but they still need peaceful fish and oxygen-rich, stable water.

Care and Setup Notes

  • Keep them in groups so they behave naturally and feed confidently.
  • Do not rely on them alone; fix the light and nutrient cause of algae too.
  • Cover intakes and escape gaps because shrimp explore relentlessly.

What to watch

Use this page as a planning profile, then match the advice to your exact tank size, livestock, temperature, filtration, and water test results. If livestock are already stressed, test ammonia, nitrite, nitrate, pH, and temperature before making big changes.
