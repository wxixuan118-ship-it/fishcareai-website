Cherry Shrimp

Neocaridina davidi · Cherry shrimp are hardy freshwater dwarf shrimp for mature planted aquariums. They graze biofilm and tiny algae, but they need peaceful tank mates and stable water more than a spotless tank.

Quick Answer

Cherry shrimp are hardy freshwater dwarf shrimp for mature planted aquariums. They graze biofilm and tiny algae, but they need peaceful tank mates and stable water more than a spotless tank.

Care and Setup Notes

  • Use a sponge pre-filter so baby shrimp are not pulled into the filter.
  • Provide moss, plants, and leaf litter for grazing surfaces.
  • Acclimate slowly because shrimp dislike sudden TDS and temperature swings.

What to watch

Use this page as a planning profile, then match the advice to your exact tank size, livestock, temperature, filtration, and water test results. If livestock are already stressed, test ammonia, nitrite, nitrate, pH, and temperature before making big changes.
