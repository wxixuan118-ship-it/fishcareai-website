African Dwarf Frog

Hymenochirus spp. · African dwarf frogs are fully aquatic amphibians that can live in peaceful freshwater aquariums. They need easy access to the surface, gentle flow, and targeted feeding because they are not fast competitors.

Quick Answer

African dwarf frogs are fully aquatic amphibians that can live in peaceful freshwater aquariums. They need easy access to the surface, gentle flow, and targeted feeding because they are not fast competitors.

Care and Setup Notes

  • Use smooth decorations and avoid narrow gaps that can trap frogs.
  • Feed with tongs or a dish if fish steal food.
  • Keep a secure lid because frogs can escape.

What to watch

Use this page as a planning profile, then match the advice to your exact tank size, livestock, temperature, filtration, and water test results. If livestock are already stressed, test ammonia, nitrite, nitrate, pH, and temperature before making big changes.
