Aquarium Resources and Recommended Fish Care Sites

FishCare AI focuses on beginner-friendly freshwater fish care. The sites below include aquarium references and broader pet resources. We link to them for information and products beyond our own guides, compatibility checker, and calculators cover. Each listing describes its scope so you can decide whether it is useful for your fish or other pets.

en.aqua-fish.net

Aqua-Fish.Net

Aqua-Fish.Net is a long-running aquarium encyclopedia with detailed profiles for hundreds of freshwater and marine fish species, plus articles on aquatic plants, invertebrates, tank maintenance, and fish diseases. It is a solid second reference for verifying tank size, temperature, pH, and temperament ranges before adding a new species.

Visit Aqua-Fish.Net

Pet Fish Directory

Pet Fish Directory

An aquarium website directory to the best aquarium and tropical fish sites. Useful for discovering species clubs, breeder pages, and hobbyist blogs that don't always surface in search results.

Visit Pet Fish Directory

caida.eu

Caida Web Directory

A general web directory with categorised listings across pets, animals, hobbies, and many other topics. Useful for discovering niche sites and small hobbyist pages that rarely appear on the first page of search results.

Visit Caida Web Directory

attractbirds.app

AttractBirds.app

AttractBirds.app is a free AI-powered bird garden planner that helps you attract the right birds with personalized recommendations for native plants, feeders, food, water, and habitat. A good companion if your interest in keeping animals extends from the aquarium out into the backyard.

Visit AttractBirds.app

aboutfishonline.com

About Fish Online

About Fish Online covers freshwater and saltwater fish profiles, invertebrates, fish health and aquarium care articles, and keeps a long-running directory of aquarium and tropical fish resources. Useful as a second reference for species care and for finding older hobbyist sites that no longer surface in search.

Visit About Fish Online

valupets.com

ValuPets

ValuPets is a UK pet supplies store with a fish section. Aquarium keepers can browse its current fish supplies and compare options before buying locally or online.

Visit ValuPets
