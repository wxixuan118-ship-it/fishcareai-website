Aquarium Resources and Recommended Fish Care Sites

FishCare AI focuses on beginner-friendly freshwater fish care. The sites below are independent aquarium references we find genuinely useful when researching species, water parameters, and tank setups. We link to them because they add depth beyond what our own guides, compatibility checker, and calculators cover.

en.aqua-fish.net

Aqua-Fish.Net

Aqua-Fish.Net is a long-running aquarium encyclopedia with detailed profiles for hundreds of freshwater and marine fish species, plus articles on aquatic plants, invertebrates, tank maintenance, and fish diseases. It is a solid second reference for verifying tank size, temperature, pH, and temperament ranges before adding a new species.
