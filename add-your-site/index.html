Add Your Aquarium Site and Link to FishCare AI

Thanks for your interest in being listed on FishCare AI. Our Resources page is a hand-picked directory of aquarium, tropical fish, and fish care websites. Listing is free. All we ask is that relevant sites add a link back to FishCare AI somewhere reachable from your site's navigation, such as a links, resources, or partners page.

How to get listed

  1. Check your site fits. We list aquarium and tropical fish related sites only: species databases, fish care blogs, aquarium clubs, breeders, forums, plant and invertebrate sites, local fish stores, aquarium software, and aquarium directories.
  2. Add a link back to us. Copy one of the text link or badge snippets below and place it on a page that is linked from your navigation. The link should be permanent and not use rel="nofollow".
  3. Send the submission form below. Tell us your site title, URL, a short description, and the reciprocal URL where our link lives. We review manually and usually reply within a week.

Listing policy: FishCare AI reserves the right to edit descriptions, choose categories, decline sites that are off-topic or low quality, and remove listings whose link back to us has been removed. We do not accept paid listings, adult, gambling, or pharmacy sites, or pages that exist only to sell links.
