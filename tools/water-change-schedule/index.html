How Often Should You Change Aquarium Water?

For most freshwater tanks, change 20–30 percent of the water once a week. Lightly stocked or heavily planted tanks can stretch to every 10–14 days; crowded tanks, goldfish and large cichlids need more, often split into two changes a week. The real test is nitrate: if it stays under 20 ppm between changes, your routine is working. If it reaches 40 ppm, change more water or change it more often.

TankTypical routineWhy
Lightly stocked community15–20% weekly, or 25% every 10–14 daysLittle waste; plants and the filter keep up.
Average community tank25% weeklyThe standard that keeps nitrate low for most stocking levels.
Heavily stocked, goldfish, cichlids30–50% a week, split into two changesBig fish make a lot of waste; nitrate climbs fast.

How the Schedule Is Worked Out

The planner estimates the bioload from the number of fish and their adult size, compared with the volume of water. Goldfish and large cichlids count double because they produce far more waste for their size. Heavy planting lowers the suggestion a little, and a nitrate reading of 20 ppm or more raises it. Any change above 30 percent is split into two smaller changes in the same week, which is gentler on fish than one big swing. These are starting points, so test nitrate before each change for the first month and adjust.

How to Do a Water Change Safely

  1. Unplug the heater and filter if the water level will drop below them.
  2. Vacuum the gravel as you siphon, working through a third to half of the substrate each time.
  3. Treat the new water with dechlorinator for the full tank volume, or follow its label.
  4. Match the temperature to within about 2°F (1°C) of the tank.
  5. Rinse filter media in the water you removed, never under the tap, and only when flow drops.

FAQ

How often should I change the water in my fish tank?

For most freshwater aquariums, 20–30% once a week. Lightly stocked or heavily planted tanks can go 10–14 days between changes, while heavily stocked tanks, goldfish and large cichlids often need 30–50% a week split into two changes.

How much water should I change in my aquarium?

Usually 20–30% at a time. Changes above about 50% can shift temperature and water chemistry too fast, so if you need more, do two smaller changes a few days apart.

Can I change too much water?

Yes, if the new water is a different temperature or chemistry from the tank. Large changes are safe when the water is dechlorinated and temperature-matched, but routine changes of 20–30% are gentler.

Should I remove fish during a water change?

No. Leave the fish in the tank. Moving them is more stressful than a partial water change.

Do I need to clean the filter at every water change?

No. Rinse filter media in removed tank water about once a month, or when flow drops. Cleaning it too often or in tap water kills the bacteria that keep ammonia at 0.
