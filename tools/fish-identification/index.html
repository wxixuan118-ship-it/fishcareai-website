Tips for Best Results

  • Use a well-lit, clear photo with the fish visible — avoid shadows and murky water
  • Fish in profile or facing the camera work best; the AI needs to see color patterns and fin shapes
  • A close-up crop is better than a wide tank shot (the AI needs to focus on the fish, not the background)
  • If unsure of the exact match, browse similar results — species within a family often share care requirements
