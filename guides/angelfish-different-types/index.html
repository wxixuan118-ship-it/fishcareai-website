Angelfish Different Types and Varieties

Freshwater angelfish varieties are color and fin morphs of the genus Pterophyllum. The most common aquarium species is Pterophyllum scalare; altum and leopoldi are less common and usually more demanding. This guide separates freshwater angelfish types from marine angelfish, which are unrelated saltwater fish.

Popular varieties

  • Marble: irregular black and white patterning.
  • Koi: orange, black and white markings.
  • Black or black lace: dark body with varying stripe patterns.
  • Gold and platinum: yellow, orange or pearly white bodies.
  • Veiltail: elongated flowing fins in many colors.

Freshwater or saltwater?

Home aquarium angelfish are freshwater fish from South America. Marine angelfish types, including queen and coral beauty angels, belong to different marine families and need saltwater reef systems. Never use a saltwater angelfish care guide for a freshwater Pterophyllum tank.

Choosing a healthy morph

Color is less important than body shape, clear eyes, open fins and active swimming. Select fish from clean systems and ask about quarantine. Some heavily selected morphs may be more sensitive to stress or bright light, but all need stable water and a suitable tall tank.

FAQ

What are the different types of freshwater angelfish?

Common types include silver, marble, koi, black, gold, platinum, albino and veiltail angelfish.

Are angelfish marine?

The angelfish kept in ordinary freshwater aquariums are not marine; marine angelfish are a separate saltwater group.
