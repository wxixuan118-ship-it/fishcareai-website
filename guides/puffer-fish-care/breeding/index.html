Puffer Fish Breeding and Sexing

🥚 Pea puffers only  |  🗓️ Hatch ~5 days  |  🐛 Infusoria first  |  ⚧ Most species unsexable

Puffers are among the hardest aquarium fish to breed, and for most species it has never been done reliably in captivity. The single exception is the pea puffer, which spawns readily in a planted tank. Almost every other puffer you can buy — green spotted, figure 8, fahaka, mbu, Amazon, congo — is wild-caught, which is why sexing and breeding information for them is so thin.

Male vs Female Puffer Fish

Most puffers show no reliable external sexual dimorphism at all. The species where you can tell:

Breeding Pea Puffers Step by Step

The pea puffer is the realistic project, and captive-bred stock genuinely matters: wild pea puffers come from a small area of south-west India under heavy collection pressure.

  1. Set up a group. One male to two or three females in a 10–20 gallon planted tank at 78–80°F, pH 7.0–7.6.
  2. Condition for two to three weeks on live blackworms, small snails and frozen bloodworms fed twice daily.
  3. Provide dense java moss or a similar fine-leaved plant across at least a third of the floor.
  4. Watch for courtship. The male darkens his belly stripe, displays and drives the female into the moss.
  5. Eggs are laid singly among the moss, roughly 5–15 per spawn, and are not guarded.
  6. Remove the adults or move the moss to a separate container — both parents eat eggs and fry.
  7. Eggs hatch in about five days at 79°F. Fry are tiny and remain on their yolk sac for a further two to three days.
  8. Feed infusoria and vinegar eels for the first week, then microworms, then baby brine shrimp from around week two.
  9. Keep the fry tank spotless. Small daily water changes with a rigid airline; fry are intolerant of any ammonia.

Puffer Fish Eggs and Fry

  • Appearance: pea puffer eggs are clear to pale amber and roughly 1 mm across — easy to miss in moss.
  • Hatch time: about five days at 79°F, longer in cooler water.
  • Fry size: under 2 mm at hatch, which is why infusoria rather than brine shrimp is the correct first food.
  • Growth: recognisably puffer-shaped by three weeks, taking baby snails by two months, adult size and sexable at around eight months.
  • Survival: expect losses. A first spawn producing five or six juveniles is a normal result.

Breeding Other Puffer Species

Realistic expectations for the rest:

  • Green spotted and figure 8 puffers: not bred in home aquariums. All stock is wild-caught from estuaries, and reproducing the migratory salinity cycle is beyond a home setup.
  • Amazon puffers: spawn seasonally in the wild with flood cycles; occasional aquarium spawns are reported but raising fry is rare.
  • Fahaka puffers: a handful of documented captive spawns, all in very large tanks. Males guard eggs on a flat surface.
  • Congo and hairy puffers: essentially unrecorded in captivity.
  • Marine puffers: pelagic spawners; larvae need a rotifer and copepod culture chain that is impractical outside a dedicated facility.

Is My Puffer Pregnant?

No — puffers are egg scatterers, not livebearers, so there is no pregnancy. A female carrying eggs looks fuller for a few days before spawning and then returns to normal. A puffer that stays swollen has a different problem: constipation, internal parasites, dropsy or trapped air. Work through the bloating and puffing up page.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can you tell a male puffer fish from a female?

Only in a few species. Mature male pea puffers have a dark stripe down the centre of the belly, iridescent wrinkle lines behind the eye, a slimmer body and brighter yellow colour. Green spotted, figure 8, fahaka and mbu puffers show no reliable external differences.

Can puffer fish breed in an aquarium?

Pea puffers breed readily in a planted tank. Fahaka puffers have spawned a handful of times in very large tanks. Green spotted, figure 8, congo and marine puffers are not bred in home aquariums, so the stock in shops is wild-caught.

How do you breed pea puffers?

Keep one male to two or three females at 78 to 80F in a planted tank, condition them on live blackworms and snails for two to three weeks, and provide dense java moss. The male drives the female into the moss to spawn, and eggs must be separated from the adults.

How long do puffer fish eggs take to hatch?

Pea puffer eggs hatch in about five days at 79F, longer in cooler water. The fry stay on their yolk sac for a further two to three days before needing infusoria or vinegar eels as a first food.

What do baby puffer fish eat?

Infusoria and vinegar eels for the first week, then microworms, then baby brine shrimp from around week two. By two months they can take crushed or newly hatched pest snails.

Can a puffer fish be pregnant?

No. Puffers scatter eggs rather than bearing live young. A female looks fuller for a few days before spawning, but a puffer that stays swollen has constipation, internal parasites, dropsy or trapped air instead.
