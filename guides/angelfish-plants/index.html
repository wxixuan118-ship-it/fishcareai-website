Best Aquarium Plants for Angelfish

A planted angelfish tank should provide cover and vertical structure without removing open swimming lanes. Angelfish come from slow, plant-rich waters, so broad leaves and tall stems can make them feel secure while also breaking sight lines during territorial disputes.

Good plant choices

  • Amazon sword for broad leaves and spawning surfaces.
  • Java fern and Anubias for low-maintenance attachment to wood.
  • Vallisneria for tall background cover.
  • Floating plants for shade, used without blocking oxygen exchange.

How to arrange plants

Keep dense growth at the back and sides, leaving a clear central lane. Use driftwood vertically to echo the structure of submerged roots. Broad leaves can become spawning sites, but leave enough access for cleaning and observation.

Lighting and maintenance

Match light intensity to plant species and provide a consistent schedule. Trim leaves that decay, vacuum around roots and avoid fertilizers that are unsafe for tank mates. Plants help absorb nutrients but do not replace filtration or water changes.

FAQ

What are the best plants for angelfish?

Amazon swords, Java fern, Anubias and Vallisneria are practical choices for many freshwater angelfish aquariums.

Do angelfish eat aquarium plants?

They may investigate leaves, but they are not dedicated plant eaters. Damaged plants are more often caused by décor, water quality or poor lighting.
