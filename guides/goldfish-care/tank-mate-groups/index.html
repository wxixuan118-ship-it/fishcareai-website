Goldfish Tank Mates: Tetras, Plecos, Shrimp and Turtles

Compare commonly proposed goldfish companions by temperature, adult size, feeding and predation risks, with links to existing compatibility guides.

FishCare AI Editorial Team · Updated September 9, 2026 · Editorial policy

Start with the exact species

Broad names such as tetra, pleco and loach cover fish with very different needs. Goldfish of similar size and swimming ability are usually the simplest starting point. Temperature overlap alone does not establish compatibility.

Common companion groups

CompanionMain concernPractical choice
TetrasMany are tropical and small enough to swallowAvoid treating the group as suitable; neon tetra comparison
PlecosDifferent adult sizes; possible rasping on goldfishDo not buy one merely as a cleaner
ShrimpAdults and young can be eatenUse separate shrimp housing

TurtlesBites, predation and high waste productionKeep in separate appropriate enclosures
MinnowsSmall fish remain prey even in cool waterPrefer a separate species-appropriate group
LoachesSize, temperature and activity differ widelyRead the weather loach and hillstream loach guides

Check the full habitat before mixing

List adult sizes, group-size requirements, feeding speed and preferred conditions for both species. Add the combined waste load and provide a second suitable tank if the pairing fails. A fish surviving for a few weeks is not evidence of long-term suitability.

Specific pairings already covered

See the goldfish tank mates directory for existing pair pages. Koi require pond-scale planning; tropical fish such as bettas, guppies and angelfish should not be added based only on a shop’s mixed display.

Frequently asked question

What are the best tank mates for goldfish?

Other compatible goldfish with similar swimming ability are often the most practical option. Choose enough space and verify that every fish eats and rests normally.
