What koi pond size do you need?

Koi pond size is measured in thousands of gallons, not tens, because koi are pond fish. Adult koi reach 24–36 inches (60–90 cm) and live 25–35+ years. They are unsuitable for indoor aquariums as permanent housing — standard aquariums are too small, lack the water volume koi need to thrive, and cannot handle the bioload of an adult fish.

This koi pond size calculator applies the standard planning rule — 250 gallons per koi minimum, 500 gallons per koi recommended — with a floor of 1,000 gallons for any pond and 2,000 gallons recommended. A starter pond for 3–4 koi should hold at least 1,000 gallons. Larger ponds — 2,000–5,000 gallons — provide more stable water quality and support more fish without the rapid decline in water parameters that plagues undersized ponds.

Pond depth matters as much as volume. Koi ponds should be at least 3 feet deep (4–5 feet is preferred) to buffer temperature swings and provide predator protection. Shallow ponds heat and cool too rapidly, and adult koi cannot overwinter safely in water less than 3 feet deep.

Indoor aquariums can house juvenile koi up to about 6–8 inches temporarily — use a 100- to 200-gallon stock tank or large aquarium as a grow-out — but plan to move them to a pond before they outgrow the space.

How many koi in a 1000 gallon pond, 2000 gallon pond or larger?

The table applies the same rule the calculator uses — 500 gallons per koi for comfortable stocking, 250 as the absolute ceiling — assuming a pond at least 3 feet deep with a filter and pump sized for the full volume every one to two hours. Counts are for adult koi at 24 inches or more; young fish look under-stocked for the first two or three years. Use the koi pond volume calculator if you only know the pond's dimensions.

Sharing the pond with other fish? Single-tail goldfish, shubunkins and golden orfe share koi water happily and count against the same volume at roughly a quarter of a koi each. Anything small becomes food. Check any pairing in the koi pond mates hub or the fish compatibility checker.

Why depth is in the number: nishikigoi (Wikipedia) are cold-water carp that overwinter at the bottom of the pond. Three feet of depth keeps that bottom layer above freezing and out of a heron's reach; a 2,000-gallon pond that is only 18 inches deep is a very large puddle, not a koi pond.

Frequently asked questions

How many gallons does a koi pond need?
The minimum koi pond size is 1,000 gallons for a small group of 3–4 koi. The rule of thumb is 250 gallons per koi at minimum and 500 gallons per koi as the comfortable standard. Larger ponds (2,000–5,000 gallons) are much easier to maintain and support a stable ecosystem.
Can koi fish live in an aquarium?
Juvenile koi under 6–8 inches can be housed temporarily in a large aquarium (100–200 gallons) as a grow-out, but adult koi belong in outdoor ponds. Adult koi reach 24–36 inches — far too large for any standard aquarium — and need the water volume, aeration, and filtration that only a proper pond can provide.
How many koi can I put in a 500-gallon pond?
A 500-gallon pond can comfortably support 1–2 koi using the 250-gallons-per-koi minimum rule. At the recommended 500 gallons per koi, a 500-gallon pond is suitable for 1 koi only. Small ponds are much harder to maintain stable water quality in, so fewer fish per gallon is always safer.
How deep does a koi pond need to be?
Koi ponds should be at least 3 feet deep, with 4–5 feet preferred. Adequate depth buffers temperature swings, protects koi from predators (herons, raccoons), and allows koi to overwinter safely in cold climates. Ponds under 3 feet deep are difficult to maintain at stable temperatures and cannot safely overwinter koi.
How many gallons per koi is the standard rule?
The commonly used standard is 250 gallons per koi as the absolute minimum and 500 gallons per koi as the comfortable recommendation. These figures assume the koi reach their full adult size of 24–36 inches. A pond stocked at 250 gallons per koi requires more frequent water changes and more powerful filtration than one stocked at 500 gallons per koi.
