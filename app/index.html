How It Works

From symptom to action in 3 steps

Tank Check combines AI observation with hard safety rules — so you get clear, prioritized guidance instead of generic advice.

1

Describe what you see

Type symptoms: lethargy, color change, clamped fins, floating, spots, or loss of appetite. Log water readings to unlock full triage.

2

Get urgency & diagnosis

Safety rules run first. Ammonia ≥ 0.25 ppm or any nitrite triggers "Acute Risk" automatically — AI cannot override these hard rules.

3

Follow the action checklist

Step-by-step tasks, ordered by priority. A Follow-up recheck is scheduled automatically to confirm recovery.

4

Track progress over time

Health timeline with before/after photos, water trends, and care history — all stored per tank, all private to you.
