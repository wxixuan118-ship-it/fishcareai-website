Email Leads Backend

This page documents how FishCare AI collects newsletter emails and contact form messages after deployment.

Collected Forms

  • newsletter: homepage email subscriptions.
  • contact: contact page messages and email addresses.

Both forms include a honeypot spam field and a submission timestamp.

Where to View Leads

  1. Open your Netlify project dashboard.
  2. Go to Forms.
  3. Choose newsletter or contact.
  4. Export submissions as CSV when needed.

Recommended Notifications

In Netlify, add an email notification so every new lead is forwarded to your inbox.

Path: Project configuration → Notifications → Form submission notifications.

Privacy Note

This admin page is marked noindex,nofollow and blocked in robots.txt. It should not be used as a public lead viewer. Actual submissions live in Netlify Forms.
