About

Practical aquarium guidance for better everyday decisions

FishCare AI is an independent educational website for freshwater and marine aquarium keepers. We publish species profiles, setup guides and interactive tools that help readers compare tank size, water conditions, feeding and compatibility.

Who publishes this site

Content is published by the FishCare AI Editorial Team. We do not claim veterinary diagnosis or undisclosed endorsements. Where individual contributors or reviewers are named in the future, their biography, role and relevant qualifications will be published alongside their work.

How we work

Pages are organized around real keeper decisions. Recommendations are checked for internal consistency, sources are credited when external media or factual references are used, and high-risk health advice directs readers to an aquatic veterinarian or qualified local professional.

Read our editorial policy, image credits and privacy policy.

Corrections and contact

Readers can report errors, request a source or suggest a topic through the contact page. Substantive corrections are reviewed before publication.
