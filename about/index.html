About FishCare AI

Making aquarium care simple, accessible, and well-documented for everyone.

Our Mission

FishCare AI makes aquarium keeping accessible. Whether you're setting up your first tank or maintaining a 500-gallon planted system, our guides, tools, and species encyclopedia help you keep fish healthy and thriving.

We believe aquarium knowledge shouldn't be scattered across 100 websites. We've consolidated care guides for 866+ species, tools for compatibility checking and tank sizing, and diagnosis guides for common diseases — all in one place, free to use.

What We Cover

  • Species Guides: Tank size, water parameters, feeding, breeding, and compatibility for each species
  • Care Guides: Step-by-step for setup, water changes, feeding schedules, and disease diagnosis
  • Free Tools: Tank size calculators, compatibility checkers, feeding schedules, water parameter scoring
  • Fish Encyclopedia: 866+ species with images, scientific names, care requirements, and personality traits
  • Disease Diagnosis: 30 common aquarium fish diseases with symptoms, causes, and treatment options

Our Promise

Every guide is researched against published aquaculture and veterinary sources. We don't sell fish, equipment, or affiliate products — we just want you to succeed with your aquarium.

We also maintain an editorial policy that governs how we write, what sources we trust, and how we handle disputes.
