About FishCare AI

Practical aquarium care for every fishkeeper

FishCare AI is an iOS app and educational website built to help aquarium keepers make better decisions — from setting up a first tank to managing an established community aquarium.

About the company

EverTrend LLC is the company that owns and operates FishCare AI. EverTrend LLC develops the FishCare AI iOS app and publishes the FishCare AI educational website at fishcareai.com. For legal notices, data requests, or business inquiries, contact us at dev@evetrend.com.

What FishCare AI is

FishCare AI started in 2026 with one goal: give freshwater aquarium keepers clear, practical guidance rather than contradictory advice scattered across forums. The site now covers more than 60 species profiles, 30+ care guides, algae control, invertebrate and amphibian care, decoration guidance, and free aquarium planning tools.

The iOS app extends that guidance to your phone — with AI-powered Tank Check that helps identify fish health problems, water quality logging, scheduled care reminders, health records, and multi-tank support. The free app tier covers one tank with core features; FishCare Plus unlocks unlimited tanks, AI photo analysis, and full health record history.

What this site covers

The guides and tools on FishCare AI are organized around the decisions a fishkeeper actually needs to make:

  • Species care — tank size, water parameters, feeding, group size, compatibility, and common health issues for freshwater and marine species
  • Tank setup — cycling a new aquarium, choosing equipment, substrate, filtration, and stocking safely
  • Water quality — understanding pH, ammonia, nitrite, nitrate, hardness, and temperature, with guidance on when and how to adjust each parameter
  • Algae control — identifying and managing brown algae, hair algae, black beard algae, green water, and cyanobacteria
  • Invertebrates and amphibians — cherry shrimp, nerite snails, mystery snails, African dwarf frogs, and other freshwater additions
  • Aquarium decoration — driftwood, aquarium sand, live plants, live rock, and coral for freshwater and marine tanks
  • Free tools — tank size calculator, compatibility checker, water parameter checker, feeding calculator, and aquarium planner

The FishCare AI Editorial Team

Content is produced by the FishCare AI Editorial Team — a group focused on aquarium husbandry accuracy, keeper-friendly language, and practical usefulness. We are not veterinarians. We do not provide individual fish diagnosis. Where a situation may require professional input, our guides direct readers to a qualified aquatic professional.

Species requirements are cross-checked against reputable husbandry, scientific, and institutional references. External images require source and attribution. Corrections submitted through the contact page are reviewed before any update is published. Our full standards are available on the editorial policy page.

Where individual contributors are added to the team in the future, their biography, role, and relevant qualifications will be published alongside their work.

Independence and accuracy

FishCare AI is independently operated. Advertising, where it appears, does not influence editorial recommendations. If sponsorships or affiliate relationships are introduced, they will be clearly disclosed near the relevant content. We do not accept undisclosed payment for guide recommendations or tool rankings.

If you find an error on this site — a wrong parameter, a species misidentified, a broken link, or a claim that seems inconsistent with established husbandry — please report it through the contact page. Substantive corrections are reviewed and published promptly.
