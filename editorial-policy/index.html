Editorial standards

How FishCare AI creates and maintains content

Purpose and authorship

Our goal is to help aquarium keepers make safer, clearer decisions. Unless a verified individual contributor is identified with a biography, articles are attributed to the FishCare AI Editorial Team. The site does not present general educational content as veterinary diagnosis.

Research and review

  • Species requirements are compared across reputable husbandry, scientific and institutional references where available.
  • Advice is checked for tank-size, water-quality, compatibility and feeding consistency.
  • External images and quoted material require source and rights information.
  • Potentially urgent health situations include a recommendation to seek qualified help.

Originality and automation

Templates and software may help organize information, but every indexed page must provide a distinct user purpose and meaningful original value. Pages that are incomplete, duplicative or awaiting review are consolidated or excluded from search indexing and advertising.

Corrections and commercial independence

Corrections can be submitted through our contact page. Advertising does not determine editorial recommendations. Sponsorships or affiliate relationships, if introduced, will be clearly disclosed near the relevant content.
